Contact Jobs GO7

Thank you for visiting Jobs GO7 at https://jobs.go7.in/. We welcome genuine questions, feedback and suggestions related to our website and the government recruitment, examination and education updates published on this portal.

Get in Touch

For website-related enquiries, corrections, source suggestions or reporting an issue with a published update, please contact the Jobs GO7 team by email:

Report an Error or Request a Correction

We take accuracy seriously and welcome specific reports that help us review and improve the information displayed on Jobs GO7. To help us understand your request, please include:

  • The title of the government job notification, result, admit card, answer key or other update.
  • The page URL on Jobs GO7 where the information appears.
  • The original official notification or issuing authority URL, wherever available.
  • A clear explanation of the information you believe needs correction.

For source suggestions, please share the name of the issuing department or organization and a link to its official website or notice.

Important Information

Jobs GO7 is an independent information portal. We do not conduct recruitment examinations, process job applications, issue admit cards or results, or make recruitment and admission decisions. For application status, eligibility decisions, examination schedules or official records, please contact the responsible department, recruitment board or examination authority directly.

Please do not send passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), bank details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information by email.
