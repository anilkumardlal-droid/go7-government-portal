GO7 Government Jobs & Exam Updates

GO7 brings together government recruitment notifications, examination results, admit cards, answer keys, admission notices, document updates, 10th and ITI opportunities, outsourcing vacancies and syllabus information. Each listing links to its original publisher. Always read the official notification before applying.

How to use GO7

Choose a category to browse relevant updates, search by department or post name, and open the original official notice for eligibility, application dates, fees and instructions. Information is provided for convenience and may change when the issuing authority publishes a correction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GO7 an official government website?

No. GO7 is an independent information portal. It is not owned by, operated by, or affiliated with any Central or State Government department, ministry, recruitment board or examination authority. Always use the original publisher website for final confirmation.

Where can I verify eligibility, fees and application dates?

Open the original official notification linked on the detail page. Check age limit, qualification, vacancies, application fee, important dates, selection process and instructions there. The issuing authority website is the final source of truth.

How often does GO7 refresh the latest updates?

The homepage checks for newly published posts automatically about once every 60 seconds while the page is open. The Refresh button also checks immediately. Source feeds themselves update according to their publisher and monitoring schedule, so a new notice may not appear instantly.

Does GO7 guarantee a job, admit card or selection?

No. GO7 does not provide jobs or guarantee applications, admit cards, examination results or selection. We provide information and links; the relevant department or examination authority controls the process.

What should I do if a listing looks incorrect or outdated?

Do not rely on the listing alone. Compare it with the latest official notice or corrigendum. Then contact GO7 with the listing URL and the original publisher URL so the information can be reviewed.

How do I open complete details for a result or recruitment update?

Select “View full details” on a post. The detail page shows the available summary, publishing source, publication date and a link to the original notice. Some details are only available on the issuing authority website.
