Privacy Policy for Jobs GO7

Website: https://jobs.go7.in/

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jobs GO7 (“we”, “our” or “us”), an independent information portal available at jobs.go7.in, handles information when you visit or contact the website. It also explains the use of cookies and how advertising technologies may operate if advertising is introduced on the website.

1. Information You Provide

Jobs GO7 currently provides an email contact option rather than a visitor account or website contact form. When you email us, we may receive the sender name, email address and the information, links or attachments that you choose to include in your message.

We use this information to read and respond to enquiries, review correction requests, consider source suggestions and address website-related matters. Please do not email passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), bank or payment details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information.

2. Technical Information and Website Security

When a website is accessed, hosting, network delivery or security providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver pages, maintain reliability and protect the service. Depending on the provider and its configuration, this may include an IP address, browser or device information, requested page and request date or time. The handling of such information by those providers is also subject to their own privacy policies.

Jobs GO7 does not currently use Google Analytics tracking code in the public website pages reviewed for this policy. If an analytics service is added in the future, this policy will be updated to identify the service and explain the relevant data practices.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies can help websites deliver features, remember settings, maintain security or support advertising. The current public website pages reviewed for this policy do not contain Google Analytics or Google advertising tags. Hosting or security providers may use technical cookies or similar technologies as part of their own service and configuration.

If Jobs GO7 introduces additional cookies, analytics or other tracking technologies, this policy will be updated to describe their purpose and provide relevant choices or information where required.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

Google AdSense advertising code is not currently installed in the public website pages reviewed for this policy. If advertising is enabled on Jobs GO7, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit the frequency of or personalise advertisements.

Google advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to show advertisements based on a visitor’s previous visits to this website or other websites. The use of these technologies and the information processed by Google are governed by Google’s own policies and settings.

  • Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Review or manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • Learn about additional choices for interest-based advertising at YourAdChoices.

Where third-party advertising vendors or ad networks are used, their identities and relevant privacy or opt-out information should be made available to visitors in accordance with applicable requirements and the advertising settings selected for the website. Advertising and consent settings will be configured before ads are served.

5. How Information May Be Shared

Jobs GO7 does not sell the contents of enquiries that visitors voluntarily send to our contact email. Information may be handled by service providers involved in delivering email, hosting, network or security services, or disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or others.

6. External Websites and Official Sources

Jobs GO7 may link to government departments, recruitment boards, educational institutions and other external websites so that visitors can consult original notices and sources. We do not control those websites. Their content, cookies and privacy practices are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Please review those policies when visiting an external website.

7. Children’s Privacy

Jobs GO7 is a general information portal and is not designed to invite children to create accounts or submit personal information. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through this website. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has sent personal information to us may contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

8. Data Retention and Security

Email correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, manage corrections, maintain relevant business records or meet legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to handle correspondence responsibly, but no method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when website features, service providers, advertising arrangements or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. Visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically.
